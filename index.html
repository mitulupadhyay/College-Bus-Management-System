How BusFlow works

Four steps from boarding to insight.

  1. Step 01

    Scan the pass

    The conductor scans a student's digital pass at the stop. Boarding is recorded against the trip in one tap.

  2. Step 02

    Stream telemetry

    Position, speed and occupancy stream to the network graph, recalculating ETA for every downstream stop.

  3. Step 03

    Route intelligence

    The engine re-runs shortest-path costs across the stop graph and flags delays before they cascade.

  4. Step 04

    Notify everyone

    Parents, students and administrators receive the same event from a single queue — no duplicated truth.
